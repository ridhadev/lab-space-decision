02. Data Provenance & Ingestion Matrix

Full Audit Trail

Every dimension used in Decision Space is auditable and classified into three strict tiers: Real API & Web Extracted, Deterministic In-Code Math, and AI-Curated & Modeled Proxies.

1. Real API & Web Extracted

Extracted directly via public web scraping (Bedashing store directory) and Google Maps APIs (Geocoding API coordinates, Places Public Star Ratings & Review Counts). 100% verified ground truth.

2. Deterministic In-Code Math

Calculated locally on-the-fly using the spherical Haversine geodesic distance formula ($R=6,371\text{ km}$), continuous cannibalization penalty curves, and multi-factor weighted scoring. No external network I/O.

3. AI-Curated & Modeled Proxies

Synthesized by the AI assistant from parametric training knowledge (training cutoff: March 2026). No live FCSC database or API was queried, and no confidential POS ledgers or CAD blueprints were accessed.

⚠️ Transparency Note: What "AI-Curated" Means in This Dataset

"Curated" means the data was synthesized using the AI's internal pre-trained domain knowledge and industry heuristics, rather than an automated database integration:

  • Catchment Affluence Index (0–100): There is no public programmatic API for the UAE Federal Competitiveness and Statistics Centre (FCSC). Instead, this score is a heuristic benchmark based on general UAE residential lease brackets (Bayut/PropertyFinder market medians: Ultra-Prime 90–95, Affluent Communities 80–86, Outer Suburban 72–78).
  • Chair Counts & Square Footage: Private lease agreements, architectural CAD floorplans, and municipality trade licenses are confidential. Counts (10 to 18 chairs) were assigned using commercial beauty industry space-planning standards (~100–120 sq ft GLA per styling station) mapped to visible venue types (boutique villas = 10–12, community centers = 14–16, regional flagships = 18).
  • Monthly Visitors: Modeled mathematically from the simulated chairs ($\text{Chairs} \times \text{Daily Turns} \times 30 \times \text{Utilization Rate}$). Actual internal POS transaction ledgers are strictly confidential.

03. Hypotheses & Strategic Assumptions

Spatial & Economic Rules

Assumption 1: 3-Kilometer Primary Catchment Buffer

In urban UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi), retail convenience for express salon services corresponds to an immediate 5-to-10 minute transit radius. Beyond 3 km, consumer retention decays significantly in favor of closer alternative salons.

Assumption 2: 4.0km Critical Sister Cannibalization Radius

When two Bedashing branches operate within < 4.0 km of each other (e.g., Delma and Khaleej Al Arabi at 1.9 km, or Noya Plaza and Westyas Plaza at 4.0 km), they divide the same local customer base without expanding the total brand revenue, diluting unit-level EBITDA.

Assumption 3: Target Consumer Demographics

Bedashing targets high-discretionary-income Emirati and expatriate women seeking premium nail, hair, lash, and beauty treatments. Thus, catchment affluence carries heavy weight in predicting lifetime customer value.

Assumption 4: Operational Proxies (Confidential POS Data Protection)

Because internal transaction records and P&L ledgers are strictly confidential, chair counts and monthly visitor counts are modeled proportionally based on venue square footage and public foot-traffic tiers.

04. Geospatial & Math Methodologies

Algorithms & Formulas

A. Spherical Haversine Geodesic Distance

Calculates the great-circle separation between coordinates $(lat_1, lon_1)$ and $(lat_2, lon_2)$ over a spherical earth of radius $R = 6,371 \text{ km}$:

a = sin²(Δlat / 2) + cos(lat₁) · cos(lat₂) · sin²(Δlon / 2)
c = 2 · atan2(√a, √(1 - a))
d = R · c

Implemented in scoringEngine.ts:calculateHaversineDistance

B. Spatial Cannibalization Penalty Curve

Transforms sister distance into a 0–100 protection score:

  • Distance < 2.0 km: Fixed severe penalty of 25 / 100.
  • Distance 2.0 km – 5.0 km: Linear interpolation: 25 + ((distance - 2.0) / 3.0) × 75.
  • Distance > 5.0 km: Full protection of 100 / 100 (Zero sister interference).

C. Competitor Saturation Normalization

Converts 3km competitor salon count into a resilience score:

  • ≤ 3 Salons: 100 / 100 (Protected trade area / local monopoly).
  • ≥ 25 Salons: 30 / 100 (Severe hyper-saturation, e.g. Jumeirah Beach corridor).
  • Between 3 and 25: Linear decay: 100 - ((competitors - 3) / 22) × 70.

D. Thermal Market Saturation & Competition Density Heatmap

Renders multi-tier concentric thermal density rings over CartoDB Dark Matter to illuminate competitive hotspots across the UAE:

  • Low Density / Moat (1–4 Salons): Blue/Cyan thermal halo (3.8km plume, 1.0km core) indicating protected trade areas (e.g. Al Dhafra, Al Ain, Saadiyat).
  • Balanced Competition (5–9 Salons): Yellow/Gold gradient representing sustainable commercial retail density.
  • High Saturation (10–17 Salons): Amber/Orange plume signaling competitive pressure (e.g. Al Khalidiya, Marina Mall AD, Al Zahia).
  • Severe Hyper-Saturation (18–26+ Salons): Deep Crimson/Red thermal center signaling margin compression and aggressive customer acquisition costs (e.g. Downtown Dubai, Jumeirah Beach Road, City Walk).

Toggleable in GeospatialMap.tsx with interactive tooltips and continuous thermal gradient legend.

E. Coverage Gap & "White Space" Opportunity Layer

Isolates unserved high-yield geographic pockets where luxury salon demand significantly outpaces current capacity without sister cannibalization:

  • Gap Classification Rule: Unmet Demand ≥ 80 AND Affluence Score ≥ 80 AND Sister Distance ≥ 3.0 km.
  • Opportunity Aura (3.5km Radius): Renders a glowing, dashed cyan/teal perimeter delineating the unserved primary trade area.
  • Unmet Demand Core (1.4km Radius): High-opacity concentric nucleus illustrating the immediate retail density hub.
  • Network Gap Reach Vector: Dotted directional vector connecting the candidate centroid to the nearest Bedashing branch, displaying exact inter-store separation and verifying zero sister cannibalization.

Toggleable in GeospatialMap.tsx with interactive deep-dive audit tooltips and capacity deficit projections.

F. Market Saturation Metric & Multi-Tier Classification System

Categorizes each branch and expansion candidate into distinct competitive saturation tiers to provide executive visibility across the portfolio:

  • Monopolistic Moat (1–4 Salons): Defensible trade territory with low competitor density and high pricing resilience.
  • Balanced Density (5–9 Salons): Equilibrium trade zone supporting steady appointments and organic repeat bookings.
  • Saturated Trade (10–17 Salons): Dense beauty market demanding continuous promotions, loyalty retention, and service differentiation.
  • Hyper-Saturated (18+ Salons): Extreme salon density with aggressive customer acquisition costs and margin compression.

Integrated into ExecutiveSummaryCards.tsx (portfolio profile strip), BranchTable.tsx & CandidateTable.tsx (dedicated sortable columns & one-click filter chips), and BranchDetailModal.tsx (metrics audit cards).

G. Collapsible Map Layers Panel & Center of Gravity Navigation

Interactive map navigation controls engineered for high-density spatial inspection and executive workflows:

  • Collapsible Floating Dialog: Allows toggling the full controls/legend panel into a compact floating badge pill (Map Layers [Emirate]) to provide an unobstructed 100% full-screen map view with one-click re-expansion.
  • Deterministic Center of Gravity: When changing the Focus Emirate (via select dropdown or quick-select chips UAE, AD, DXB, SHJ, FUJ, RAK), the camera calculates the arithmetic centroid $(\bar{\text{lat}}, \bar{\text{lng}})$ of all selected branches and candidate growth zones.
  • Calibrated Symmetrical Zoom: Fits symmetric geographic bounds around the Center of Gravity with proportional margins, guaranteeing the map is centered on trade mass while accommodating full spatial spread (zoom 8 for UAE, ~10 for Abu Dhabi & Al Ain, ~11 for Dubai Metro, ~12 for Sharjah, ~12.5 for single-location emirates like Fujairah and Ras Al Khaimah).
  • Unobstructed Bottom-Right Zoom Controls: Relocated zoom in/out (+ / −) controls from underneath the top-left layer dialog to the bottom-right corner of the map viewport, styled in dark navy glassmorphism with high-contrast active states.

Implemented in GeospatialMap.tsx with smooth Leaflet flyToBounds and flyTo transitions.

05. Deterministic Scoring Engine

Classification Rules

Branch Health Index Formula

Calculated as a calibrated weighted sum across 4 dimensions:

Final Score = (Reputation × W_rep + Affluence × W_aff + Distance × W_dist + Competition × W_comp) / TotalWeight
PROTECT (Score ≥ 85) Crown-jewel flagships with strong moats and high ratings. Defend with priority CapEx.
HOLD (Score 70 – 84) Stable neighborhood earners. Optimize chair throughput and appointment efficiency.
SHRINK (Score < 70) Underperforming or heavily cannibalized sites. Downsize chairs or consolidate lease.

06. AI Advisory & Board Memorandum

Zero Hallucination

Strict Grounding Protocol

The AI Advisor does not invent scores or ratings. Prompts injected into Gemini include exact mathematical scores, review counts, competitor densities, and sister distances computed by the deterministic engine.

Dual-Path Resilience (Gemini 3.8 Flash + Deterministic Fallback)

If upstream Google Gemini models encounter 503 high-demand spikes or network rate limits, server.ts activates an automated exponential backoff. If all retries fail, it falls back to the internal Deterministic Grounded Generator, ensuring continuous 200 OK responses with zero downtime.

Rich Markdown Presentation Pipeline

All AI generated output (natural language strategic explanations, board memorandums, and chat conversations) are parsed via MarkdownRenderer.tsx leveraging react-markdown and GitHub Flavored Markdown (remark-gfm). Replaces raw text dumps with high-contrast typography, styled H1–H4 hierarchy, structured tables, formatted bulleted lists, and monospace code blocks.

Per-Branch/Candidate Local Storage Caching & Loading States

AI recommendations are isolated per branch and candidate using /src/services/aiStorage.ts. If an AI deep dive has never been requested for a specific location, an explicit generation prompt is presented. Upon execution, an active processing animation displays a pulsing radar indicator, status text, and shimmering skeleton placeholders. Once returned, rationales are persisted in browser localStorage with timestamps and instant zero-latency retrieval on subsequent visits, alongside one-click regeneration controls.
